Eligibility Guide

Can You Donate Blood?

Most healthy adults are eligible to donate blood. Check the basic guidelines below:

Age between 18 and 65 years

Donors must be in good physical health and sound mind.

Weight 45 kg or above

Adequate body weight ensures safe fluid withdrawal during donation.

Hemoglobin Level ≥ 12.5 g/dL

Ensures you have healthy oxygen-carrying capacity.

Donation Gap of 90 Days

Minimum 3 months interval since your last whole blood donation.

Quick Compatibility Facts

  • O-
    Universal Red Blood Cell Donor: Can donate to ANY blood group in emergencies.
  • AB+
    Universal Recipient: Can receive blood from ANY blood group safely.
  • Rh+
    Rh Positive: Can receive from both Rh+ and Rh- matching groups.
  • Rh-
    Rh Negative: Can receive blood ONLY from Rh- groups.
